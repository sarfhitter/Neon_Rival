Touch controls are available below the arena. Choose your fighter, then explicitly choose the AI opponent. The bot difficulty can be Rookie, Rival, or Elite.

Each battle lasts 90 seconds. A knockout wins immediately; at time-out, the fighter with the higher percentage of health wins. Equal health percentages produce a draw.

Training lab

Select your fighter and a roster character to serve as the stationary dummy. The dummy never attacks or blocks. Energy stays full, health recovers after hits, and the clock never runs out. Specials retain their cooldowns.

Kaosar still burns out after six seconds of active combat. Use Reset Training to restore his power window and reset positions, health, and effects.
